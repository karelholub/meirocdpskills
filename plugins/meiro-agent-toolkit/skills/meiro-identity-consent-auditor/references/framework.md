# Identity and consent framework

- Identifier contract: purpose, format, normalization, scope and owner.
- Resolution: precedence, confidence, conflict and split/merge policy.
- Lifecycle: anonymous creation, identification, logout, cross-device and deletion.
- Consent: purpose, channel, status, source, timestamp, version and withdrawal.
- Enforcement: collection, audience eligibility, delivery, export and suppression.
- Governance: lawful-basis owner, retention, access, deletion and audit evidence.

Do not infer legal compliance. Report technical evidence and decisions requiring privacy/legal ownership.
