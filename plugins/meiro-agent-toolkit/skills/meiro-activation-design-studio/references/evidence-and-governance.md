# Evidence and governance

Read this reference for regulated activations, uncertain service/marketing purpose, exact claims/legal copy, or public-site brand evidence.

## Evidence states

- `OBSERVED_VISUAL`: visible in a dated screenshot or rendered source.
- `OBSERVED_CODE`: extracted from public CSS/markup or an approved source file.
- `PROVIDED`: supplied by the user or an approved brief; approval still needs an explicit state.
- `PROPOSED`: a design interpretation or new direction.
- `UNKNOWN`: not established.

Never treat a sampled screenshot color as an exact brand token unless an approved asset/style source confirms it. Record approximation method and confidence.

## Brand evidence pack

Capture only what changes the activation design:

- logo/lockup rules and clear-space evidence;
- primary/supporting colors with source and confidence;
- type families, hierarchy and fallback fonts;
- grid, spacing, radii, border, shadow and button patterns;
- image crop, lighting, subject, illustration and icon style;
- content density, heading grammar, pronouns, punctuation and CTA verbs;
- service versus promotional treatment;
- legal/disclaimer placement;
- accessibility and localization patterns.

Do not copy a site's entire stylesheet or creative library. Cite the smallest useful source evidence and create original activation compositions.

## Governed brief precedence

The campaign/journey plan controls:

1. purpose and communication classification;
2. audience and consent;
3. inclusions, exclusions and stop conditions;
4. personalization fields;
5. timing/frequency/priority;
6. measurement/holdout;
7. rollback and approval state.

Design may surface a contradiction but must not resolve it by changing the brief. Record a blocking question instead.

## Exact-content rules

- `EXACT`: render verbatim from an approved source; no paraphrase.
- `DRAFT`: model-generated proposal awaiting review.
- `UNKNOWN`: required content not supplied; use a labeled placeholder in internal mockups only.
- `PROHIBITED_TO_INVENT`: price, coverage, eligibility, underwriting/claim outcome, legal approval, certification, regulated claim, endorsement, expiry or scarcity not established by an authoritative source.

For exact content, record source, owner, locale, version, allowed transformation, and where it must appear. A design cannot turn `DRAFT` into `EXACT`.

## Regulated and sensitive contexts

- Keep raw claim, health, vulnerability, fraud, payment, credential, document, precise-location and companion/minor data out of creative and personalization.
- Use generic status references and secure links for service flows.
- Do not imply the CDP chose a product, price, eligibility, claim outcome, or advice.
- Keep service creative free of unrelated marketing unless the governing policy explicitly permits it.
- Provide a visible human-help route when the brief requires it.

## Approval boundary

The bundle must keep these false by default:

- brand approval;
- legal/compliance approval;
- business/content approval;
- production readiness;
- activation approval;
- Meiro/website/channel writes.

Record reviewers and open questions without asserting their approval.
